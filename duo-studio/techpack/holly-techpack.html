Construction

  • Overlock seams with coverstitch topstitching where visible; clean finish inside.
  • Keyhole edges at centre front: clean self-fabric facing, no raw edges.
  • Bows must hold their shape after washing (secure attachment, bar-tack at tie base).
  • Peplum gathers evenly distributed; waist seam stabilised to prevent stretching.
  • Pre-shrunk fabric. Maximum shrinkage 3 % after 5 washes at 30 °C.

Testing before approval

  • Opacity (Soft White), colour fastness (Cherry Wine must not bleed onto light colours), pilling, shrinkage after 5 washes, stretch recovery of rib and waistband.
  • Fit on at least five body types.
